      Why nothing here gets stored
      
This page has no server behind it. When you click "Prepare email," your browser builds a standard mailto: link from what you've typed and hands it to your device's own email app — the same as clicking someone's email address on any website. Nothing you type is sent to us or stored anywhere online. If you tick "Remember my name and address," that's saved only in this browser on this device — never sent anywhere — and you can remove it any time by ticking "Forget my details." The email that goes out is sent from your own account, so it genuinely comes from you — it isn't relayed or spoofed by any third party.
